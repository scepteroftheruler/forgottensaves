# ForgottenSaves

**Find what you saved on Instagram by when you saved it.**

Instagram doesn't show when you saved a post, which makes old saves hard to find. ForgottenSaves is a Chrome extension that records the date for you, so you can jump back to any month or year.

**[Add to Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/forgottensaves/jejcmhdagjfnmkjpjieiffpfchponlbo)** · Free · Your data never leaves your browser.

## How it works

1. Install the extension and keep using Instagram on instagram.com as usual.
2. Each time you save a post, ForgottenSaves records the date and time, the link, the author, the start of the caption and a small thumbnail.
3. Click the extension icon to open your list. Pick a month and year, or search by author or caption.

## Features

- The real date and time you saved each post (not the date it was posted)
- Browse by month and year, search by author or caption
- Thumbnails stay visible even after Instagram's image links expire
- Posts you later removed from your saved list are marked
- Back up and restore your list as a JSON file

## Good to know

- It only records posts you save **after installing**. Older saves can't be dated.
- It only sees saves made in the browser on instagram.com. Saves made in the Instagram mobile app aren't detected.
- Everything is stored locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.
- If Instagram changes how its site works, the extension may need an update.
