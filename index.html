ForgottenSaves

Find what you saved by when you saved it.

Instagram doesn’t show when you saved a post. ForgottenSaves keeps the date for you, so you can go back to any month or year.

Add to Chrome Free. Your data never leaves your browser.

How it works

  1. Install the extension and keep using Instagram on instagram.com as usual.
  2. Each time you save a post, ForgottenSaves records the date, the link, the author, the start of the caption and a small thumbnail.
  3. Click the extension icon to open your list. Pick a month and year, or search by author or caption.

What you get

  • The real date and time you saved each post, not the date it was posted.
  • A list you can filter by month and year, and search by author or caption.
  • Thumbnails that stay visible even after Instagram’s image links expire.
  • A mark on posts you later removed from your saved list.
  • Back up and restore your list as a file, so you never lose it.

Good to know

  • It only records posts you save after installing. Older saves can’t be dated.
  • It only sees saves made in the browser on instagram.com. Saves made in the Instagram mobile app aren’t detected.
  • Everything is stored on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. Read the privacy policy.
  • If Instagram changes how its site works, the extension may need an update.
