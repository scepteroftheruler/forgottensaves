# ForgottenSaves Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 13, 2026

ForgottenSaves ("the extension") is a browser extension that remembers when you saved posts on Instagram and lets you browse them by month and year. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how.

ForgottenSaves is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram or Meta Platforms, Inc.

## Summary

- Everything the extension saves stays **on your own device**, inside your browser.
- The developer **does not receive, collect, or have access to** any of your data.
- Your data is **never sold, shared, or sent to any server** operated by the developer or any third party.
- The extension contains **no analytics, tracking, or advertising**.

## What data the extension handles

When you save or unsave a post on instagram.com while the extension is installed, it records the following information about that post:

- The post's ID and link
- The author's username, when it can be read from the page
- The beginning of the post's caption (up to 300 characters), when it can be read from the page
- A small, downsized copy of the post's thumbnail image
- The date and time you saved the post, and the date and time you removed it (if you did)

The extension does not read your messages, your password, your followers, your browsing history outside of instagram.com, or any other personal information.

## Where the data is stored

All of this information is stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB storage, in the extension's own storage area. It is not uploaded anywhere. If you clear the extension's data or uninstall the extension, the stored information is deleted.

## Network requests the extension makes

The extension does not communicate with any server run by the developer. The only network requests it makes are to Instagram's own image servers (such as cdninstagram.com and fbcdn.net), to download a copy of a post's thumbnail so it can still be shown later if the original link expires. These requests go directly from your browser to Instagram.

## Why the extension needs its permissions

- **Access to instagram.com:** to detect when you save or unsave a post and to read the basic post details listed above from the page you are viewing.
- **Access to Instagram's image servers (cdninstagram.com, fbcdn.net):** to download and store a small copy of the thumbnail of a post you saved.

The extension uses this access only for its single purpose: keeping a dated list of the posts you save on Instagram.

## Backups and exports

The extension offers a "Back up (JSON)" button that exports your saved list to a file on your own device, and a "Restore" button that imports such a file. These actions happen entirely on your device. Where you keep the backup file is up to you.

## Your control over your data

You can delete any entry from the list using the "Delete from list" button, remove everything by clearing the extension's data in your browser, or uninstall the extension at any time. Deleting an entry from the extension's list does not remove the post from your Instagram saved collection.

## Data sharing and use

The developer does not sell or transfer user data to third parties. User data is not used or transferred for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose, and it is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. The extension's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Children's privacy

The extension does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect or transmit data to the developer at all.
